Help improve the AI

A photo is kept every 15 checks, and on every check where the AI thinks it sees a failure. Photos you send go to the Printer Buddy dataset and make the next model better. Nothing is sent while sending is off.

Automatic sending labels each print's photos from how it ended: a finished print's photos as "looks fine" (anything the AI flagged was a false alarm), a cancelled or failed print's flagged photos with what the AI saw. Your own labels are never changed.
